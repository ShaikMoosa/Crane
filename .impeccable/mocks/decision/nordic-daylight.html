Designing a quieter workspace

Edited today at 10:42#work/studio#ideas

A quiet workspace is not an empty one. It is a place where the next action is obvious and everything else knows how to wait.

## Keep the useful edges

The desk can still hold references, tools, and half-formed thoughts. The difference is hierarchy: writing stays bright while navigation recedes into daylight.

  • Keep today’s note in reach.
  • Let finished work move out of sight.
  • Use #tags where the thought naturally belongs.

Good calm is active. It protects attention without hiding capability.

# a tiny rule for the room
attention = visible_work - visual_noise
