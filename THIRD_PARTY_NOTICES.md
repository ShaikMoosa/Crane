## Lucide icons (vendored subset)

Crane vendors a subset of [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide) icons at release **1.60.0** under `apps/desktop/assets/icons/lucide/`, with per-file SHA-256 hashes in `apps/desktop/assets/icons/lucide/manifest.json`. The release archive `lucide-icons-1.60.0.zip` has SHA-256 `1d5adc7745d4439235570841b39e82dbb2bdcbb4d57893fd183dd46ac56df9a8`. Icons are embedded in the binary and rendered offline; nothing is downloaded at build or runtime beyond the one-time vendoring step.
